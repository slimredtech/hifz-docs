## Reasons for each choice (for your review)

- **"Users Interact" = Yes.** The teacher's note is free text from one user to another, so the
  honest answer is yes even though it is narrow. Answering no and having a reviewer find the note
  is a bigger risk than the label.
- **13+ rather than "all ages".** Targeting under-13s would bring the Families policy (certified
  SDKs only, no Google sign-in prompts for children, stricter data rules). The app suits young
  memorizers too, but with a parent; the policy says it is not designed for children.
