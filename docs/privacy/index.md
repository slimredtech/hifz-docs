# Privacy policy

**English** · [Français](fr/) · [العربية](ar/)

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

Hifz Planner ("خطة الحفظ", "the app") helps you plan and review your Quran memorization. It is
developed by beyapps ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, where it is
stored, who can see it, and how to delete it.

**In short:** you do not need to give your name or email. The app creates an anonymous account so
your progress is backed up. We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and there is no analytics or
advertising SDK in the app.

## 1. Data the app uses

### Stored on our server (Supabase)

| Data                                 | What it is                                                                                                       | Why                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account identifier                   | A random ID created on first launch (anonymous account). It contains no personal information.                    | To back up and sync your progress                                                       |
| Google account (only if you link it) | Your email address and the basic profile Google provides (name, profile picture link), and the Google account ID | To sign you in on another phone; the app shows only the email                           |
| Memorization progress                | For each page: whether it is memorized, when, its review schedule and your last rating                           | To plan your daily reviews                                                              |
| Review history                       | The page, date, type and rating (Good / Hard / Forgot) of each review                                            | Scheduling, streak and statistics                                                       |
| Settings                             | Daily targets, memorization direction, streak, and the prayer or time chosen for your reminder                   | So your settings follow you to a new phone                                              |
| Activity days                        | The days you used the app, the number of reviews and whether the day's plan was completed                        | Streak across phones; to measure, in aggregate, whether the app helps people keep going |
| Display name (optional)              | A name you may choose for teachers and partners (40 characters at most)                                          | Shown only to people you link with                                                      |
| Teacher and partner links (optional) | Who you are linked with, invite codes, weekly assignments (pages and a note of up to 140 characters)             | Teacher mode and accountability partner                                                 |
| Premium status                       | Whether Premium is active, the product and its expiry date                                                       | To unlock Premium features                                                              |

**Stays on your phone only:** the city or coordinates of your reminder, your language, theme and
display options, backup files you export, and your scheduled notifications. Prayer times are
calculated on the phone.

**Technical data:** like any online service, our hosting provider processes your IP address and
request logs to deliver and protect the service. We do not use them to identify you or to work
out your location, and they are kept only for a limited period set by the provider.

### Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled entirely by **Google Play**; we never see your card details. We use
**RevenueCat** to check your purchases. It receives your random account identifier and your
purchase history (product, dates, price, country/currency). See RevenueCat's privacy policy:
<https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

### Mushaf pages (GitHub)

The pages of the mushaf are downloaded from **GitHub**, which hosts a public copy of the page
fonts, when you choose to download them or the first time you open a page. Like any website, GitHub
receives your IP address and the name of the file requested. The app sends nothing else: no account
identifier and no personal data. See GitHub's privacy statement:
<https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>.

### Quran recitations (Tarteel, QuranicAudio and EveryAyah)

When you listen to a recitation in the reader, or download one to listen offline, the audio comes
from the servers that publish it: **Tarteel** (`audio-cdn.tarteel.ai`, for Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary
and Mishary Rashid Alafasy), **QuranicAudio** (`download.quranicaudio.com`, for Maher
Al-Muaiqly) and **EveryAyah** (`everyayah.com`, for the Warsh recitations of Yassin Al-Jazaery and
Ibrahim Al-Dosary). Like any website, they receive your IP address and the name of the file requested.
The app sends nothing else: no account identifier and no personal data. Downloaded recitations stay
on your phone and are not included in your backups; you can remove them in Settings > Offline
recitations. Privacy policies: Tarteel <https://tarteel.ai/privacy>, QuranicAudio
<https://quranicaudio.com/privacy.html>; EveryAyah does not publish one.

## 2. Who can see your data

- **You.** Your data is protected by your account: no other user can read it.
- **Your teacher**, only if you join one with an invite code and accept the consent screen: the
  pages you memorized, the date and rating of each review, your streak and active days, and your
  display name. Never your settings, email or Google account. You can stop sharing at any time
  (Settings > Premium and sharing > Teacher and partner); access ends immediately.
- **Your accountability partner**, only if you link with one: your display name, your streak,
  whether today is done, and your last active day. Never your pages, reviews or email.
- **Service providers** that process data for us: Supabase (database and sign-in, servers in Ireland,
  European Union), RevenueCat (purchase status) and Google (sign-in and payments). They may use it
  only to provide their service to us.
- Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We do **not** sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

## 3. What we do not do

- No ads and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. (Google Play may give us anonymous crash
  statistics, "Android vitals", as it does for every app.)
- No access to your contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
- No public profiles and no messaging between users (only the teacher's short weekly note).

## 4. Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each table on the server is protected by
row-level security, so a user can reach only their own data and what others explicitly shared
with them. Secret keys never ship in the app.

## 5. How long we keep data

We keep your data as long as your account exists. Invite-code attempts are deleted after one
day. When you delete your account, everything listed in section 1 is deleted from our server
immediately and permanently.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Delete everything:** Settings > Account and backup > **Delete my account and data**. Without
  the app, see <https://slimredtech.github.io/hifz-docs/delete-account/>.
- **Export your data:** Settings > Account and backup > Export a backup file (a JSON file you keep).
- **Stop sharing** with a teacher or partner at any time.
- **Notifications:** you can turn the reminder off in Settings or in Android settings.
- Depending on where you live (for example the GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access,
  correct, delete or move your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to
  us to exercise these rights.

Purchases: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play >
Payments & subscriptions.

## 7. Children

The app is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in
the app.
